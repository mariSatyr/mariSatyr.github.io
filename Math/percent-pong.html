Key ideas

error = |measured − actual|
percent = (part ÷ whole) × 100
percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Always divide by the original (actual) amount.

5) Living room: 21.7 feet

a. Round 21.7 to the nearest foot. The tenths digit is 7 (5 or more), so round up.

Show answer a

22 feet

b. Find the difference.

Show answer b
22 − 21.7 = 0.3

0.3 feet

c. Error ÷ actual length.

Show answer c
0.3 ÷ 21.7 ≈ 0.0138

≈ 1.4%

6) Restaurant bill: $38.00, total $44.27

a. Total minus bill gives the tip.

Show answer a
44.27 − 38.00 = 6.27

$6.27

b. Tip ÷ bill (before tip).

Show answer b
6.27 ÷ 38 = 0.165

16.5%

7) Price of silver: $6.82 → $15.97

Find the increase, then divide by the old price.

Show answer
Increase: 15.97 − 6.82 = 9.15
9.15 ÷ 6.82 ≈ 1.342

≈ 134.2% increase

Check: 6.82 × 2.342 ≈ 15.97 ✔
