Volume Visualizer

Pick a shape, change its measurements and see its volume worked out. Then compare every shape side by side as water pours in.

Drag sideways on the shape to turn it. The water level is a share of the shape’s volume.

Every shape gets the same radius r and height h (the sphere and hemisphere use r only, and the box and pyramid have a base 2r wide). Water flows into all of them at the same rate, so a shape that holds less fills sooner. Press “Set h = 2r” to see the classic result: a cone holds 1/3 and a sphere holds 2/3 of a cylinder’s volume.
