Cone

A cone is a 3D shape with a circular base and a point at the top (apex). The height is measured from the base to the apex.

Volume = (1/3)πr²h
Surface Area = πr² + πrl
(where l = slant height)

Sphere

A sphere is a perfectly round 3D shape. Every point on its surface is the same distance (radius) from the center.

Volume = (4/3)πr³
Surface Area = 4πr²
