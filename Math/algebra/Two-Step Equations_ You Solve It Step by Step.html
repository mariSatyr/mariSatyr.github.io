The plan that works every time

  • Do the same thing to both sides.An equation is a balanced scale.
  • Undo the last thing that was done to the variable.Usually that's adding or subtracting first, then multiplying or dividing. If the whole left side is in parentheses or over a fraction bar, undo that multiplying or dividing first.
  • Use the opposite operation.Add cancels subtract. Multiply cancels divide.
