Instructions / Tasks for students

  1. Choose a function (or enter a custom expression) and domain. Click 'Plot'.
  2. Click 'Mark extrema' to estimate absolute and local extrema on the domain.
  3. Write the range in set-builder notation in the box and click 'Check set-builder'. The app accepts inequalities like -1 <= y <= 4, interval notation like [-1,4], and will check approximately.
  4. Pick x1 and x2 and click 'Show secant & compute slope' to see the average rate of change between the two points (slope of secant line).
  5. Teachers: enable teacher mode to reveal correct set-builder and to label extrema answers.
