Interactive: Absolute/Local Max & Min, Set-Builder, Average Rate of Change

Pick a function, set an x-interval, and use the tools to explore absolute maxima/minima, local maxima/minima, write the range in set-builder form, and compute the average rate of change (AROC) between two x-values.

Instructions / Tasks for students

  1. Choose a function and domain. Click 'Plot'.
  2. Click 'Mark extrema' to have the app estimate absolute and local extrema on the domain.
  3. Write the range in set-builder notation in the box and click 'Check set-builder'. The app will check a standard normalized form (simple comparisons like y ≤ c, y >= c).
  4. Pick x1 and x2 and click 'Show secant & compute slope' to see the average rate of change between the two points (slope of secant line).
