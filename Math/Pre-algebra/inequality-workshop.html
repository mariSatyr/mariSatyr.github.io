Keep the sign

Adding, subtracting, or multiplying/dividing both sides by a positive number leaves the sign alone.

Flip the sign

Multiply or divide both sides by a negative number and the sign reverses.

< ↔ >   ≤ ↔ ≥

Graph it

< or >: open circle ○ (the number is not included).
≤ or ≥: closed circle ● (the number is included).
The arrow points to the numbers that work.
