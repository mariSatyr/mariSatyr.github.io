The Crock Pot Caper: how to crack each clue

Pick a problem, try it on paper first, then reveal one step at a time to check your thinking. This guide shows the answer and its graph, but not the code letters. Dale still needs you to do that part.

Clear parenthesesDistribute the number outside to every term inside, signs included.
Combine like termsTidy up each side. Then move variables to one side, numbers to the other.
Isolate the variableDivide by the number stuck to the variable.
Negative? Flip it.Multiply or divide by a negative and the inequality sign turns around.
