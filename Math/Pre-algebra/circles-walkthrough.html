Circles, one step at a time

Pick a problem. Answer each small step to unlock the next one. Stuck? A wrong answer gives you a hint, and you can always ask to see the step.

Calculator tip: use the π key, not 3.14, so your answers match the answer choices. You can type answers like 31.4, 10pi or 10π.
