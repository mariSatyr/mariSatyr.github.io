The rock cycle is Earth's continuous natural process where rocks constantly transform between three primary types: Igneous, Sedimentary, and Metamorphic.

Quick Geology Fact

Rocks don't stay the same forever! Over millions of years, heat, pressure, weathering, and plate tectonics recycle every rock on Earth's crust.

COOLING

Crystallization & Cooling

Occurs when magma or lava cools down. Mineral molecules organize into repeating geometric lattice structures forming igneous rock.

Magma → Igneous Rock
BREAKDOWN

Weathering & Erosion

Water, wind, and ice break rocks down into small pieces (weathering) and transport them away to new locations (erosion).

Rock → Sediments
PRESSURE

Compaction & Cementation

Layers of sediment build up over time. Deep weight squeezes out water (compaction) and minerals glue grains together (cementation).

Sediment → Sedimentary Rock
METAMORPHISM

Heat & Sub-surface Pressure

Intense underground thermal energy and tectonic squeezes alter mineral composition without completely melting the rock.

Any Rock → Metamorphic Rock
TECTONICS

Tectonic Tension & Stress

Tension is the stress force that pulls Earth's crust in opposite directions at divergent tectonic boundaries, creating faults and rifts.

Crustal Deformity
ENERGY

Seismic Magnitude & Energy

Earthquake magnitude directly measures the total amount of energy released when rocks break along tectonic faults.
