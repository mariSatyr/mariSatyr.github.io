How the PK Elimination Model Works:

Caffeine is metabolized with an average half-life of ~5.8 hours in healthy adults, though it can range from 3 to 9 hours based on genetics, liver enzymes (CYP1A2), and medications. The model sums the exponential decay of each dose over time ($C(t) = \sum C_{0,i} \cdot 0.5^{(t - t_i)/h}$).
