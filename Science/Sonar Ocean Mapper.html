Mission: find all six features

Use as few pings as you can, then check your 2D and 3D maps. Try “Fill gaps” to see how scientists estimate between readings.

  • Continental shelfShallow (under about 200 m) and gently sloping, next to land.
  • Continental slopeDepth drops quickly over a short distance.
  • Abyssal plainWide, flat, and deep (about 4,000 m).
  • TrenchA narrow strip much deeper than the plain around it.
  • Mid-ocean ridgeA long underwater mountain chain rising from the plain.
  • SeamountA single underwater mountain that does not reach the surface.
