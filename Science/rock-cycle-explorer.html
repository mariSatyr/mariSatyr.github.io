Part 2 · Learn

The three types of rock

Scientists group rocks by how they form. Tap an example rock to learn about it.

Igneous

“Born from fire”

How it forms
Melted rock cools and hardens. Slow cooling underground (intrusive) makes big crystals. Fast cooling at the surface (extrusive) makes tiny crystals or glass.
Clues
Crystals, a glassy shine, or gas-bubble holes

Tap an example above.

Sedimentary

“Made of layers”

How it forms
Bits of rock, sand, mud and shells settle in layers. Over time they are pressed together (compaction) and glued by minerals (cementation).
Clues
Layers, grains you can see, and fossils

Tap an example above.

Metamorphic

“Changed form”

How it forms
Any rock buried deep underground gets squeezed and heated. It changes without melting, and its minerals line up in new ways.
Clues
Stripes or bands (foliation), hard and often shiny
