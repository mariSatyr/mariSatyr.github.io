Part 2 · Discover

Three types of rock

All rocks fall into one of three categories based on how they form. As rocks move through the cycle, they change from one type to another.

Igneous

"Fire-formed"

Forms when
Magma cools and crystallizes
Large crystals = slow cooling underground
Small crystals = fast cooling at surface

Sedimentary

"Pressed pieces"

Forms when
Layers of sediment are compacted and cemented
Visible layers and grains
Often contains fossils

Metamorphic

"Changed form"

Forms when
Rock is buried deep and exposed to heat and pressure
Banded or striped patterns
Super dense and hard
