Key Weathering Principles

  • Chemical Weathering: Breakdown through chemical reactions (e.g., acid precipitation reacting with Calcium Carbonate in limestone).
  • Physical Erosion: Mechanical breakdown via frost wedging, wind sandblasting, and river sediment abrasion.
  • Hardness Impact: Rocks higher on Mohs scale (like Quartz in Granite) withstand physical wear longer than softer rocks.
