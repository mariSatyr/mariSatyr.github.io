## Representative-image notes

These are not missing items — each has a PD/CC0 image — but the image is representative rather than a literal scene, because no PD/CC0 image of the literal subject was found (the literal images on Commons are CC-BY/SA):

- Molten Earth: NASA artist's concept of the early Earth with volcano/ocean/lightning, not a fully molten globe.
- First microbes: modern *E. coli* SEM as a representative microbe; no PD image of actual Archean microfossils was found.
- Stromatolites: Cambrian-age stromatolites (Pika Formation, Banff), not Archean-aged ones.
- Ediacaran biota: represented by *Charnia sp.*, a genuine Ediacaran organism.
- Brachiopods and corals: shows an Ordovician brachiopod only; no PD image showing brachiopods and corals together was found.
- Jawless fish: small 1911 restoration drawing (1063×195 px).
- First forests: an *Archaeopteris* fossil specimen, not a forest scene.
- The Great Dying: NASA view of the Siberian Traps landscape (Putorana Plateau), not the extinction event itself.
- First mammals: *Cynognathus*, a Triassic cynodont mammal ancestor; no PD/CC0 image of an actual first mammal (*Morganucodon* / *Hadrocodium*) exists on Commons.
- Flowering plants: a PD botanical plate of magnolia in flower, not a Cretaceous flower fossil.
- Triceratops: low resolution (600×395 px) — the best PD single-subject life restoration available.
- Titanoboa: fossil vertebra photo, not a life reconstruction.
- Australopithecus: the Mrs. Ples fossil skull photo, not a life reconstruction.
- Megalodon: skeletal diagram, not a life restoration.
- Homo sapiens: the Herto fossil skull, not a modern-human portrait.
