Genetics & Heredity

Genetics covers the chemistry of heredity (DNA/RNA), how traits are inherited, and how modern tools like PCR and sequencing help us read genomes. Example topics:

  • DNA structure & replication
  • Mendelian crosses & Punnett squares
  • Chromosomes, meiosis, and genetic variation

Ecology & Ecosystems

Ecology examines relationships between organisms and their environment. Core concepts include trophic levels, energy flow, nutrient cycles, and human-driven change.

  • Populations & carrying capacity
  • Food webs & energy pyramids
  • Conservation and biodiversity

Evolution & Diversity

Evolution explains the diversity of life through variation, selection, and time. The section covers evidence, mechanisms, and phylogenetic thinking.
