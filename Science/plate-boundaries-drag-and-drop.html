Divergent

Plates move APART

The crust stretches and cracks open. Look for rift valleys, gorges and new volcanic land in the gap.

Convergent

Plates move TOGETHER

Plates crash and crumple, or one dives under the other. Look for towering mountains and volcanoes.

Transform

Plates SLIDE SIDEWAYS

Plates grind past each other along a fault. Look for a straight line sliced through the land — offset valleys and scarps.
