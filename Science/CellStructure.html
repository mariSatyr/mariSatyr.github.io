Introduction to Cell Structure

Cells are the basic units of life. This module introduces organelles and their functions, differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, and microscopy basics.

[Cell diagram placeholder — add an image named 'cell_diagram.png' in /Science if you want a visual]

Key Organelles

  • Nucleus — stores DNA and controls cellular activities.
  • Mitochondria — produces ATP via cellular respiration.
  • Ribosomes — synthesize proteins.
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum — rough ER for protein folding; smooth ER for lipid synthesis.
  • Golgi Apparatus — processes and packages proteins.
  • Cell Membrane — selective barrier controlling transport.

Microscopy Activity

Observe and sketch onion epidermis cells or cheek cells under a microscope. Label organelles you can identify, and record magnification and stain used.
